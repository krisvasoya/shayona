# Invoice Bill Maker - User Manual (Shop Owner's Guide) 📖

Welcome to the **Invoice Bill Maker App**! This application is designed specifically for retail shop owners to manage bills, track customer dues (Baki), payments (Jama), generate clean PDF receipts, and share invoices directly on WhatsApp with zero tax/GST complexity.

---

## 1. Login & Getting Started
- Open the app on your phone.
- Tap **"Sign in with Google"** and select your Google account.
- Alternatively, you can use **"Quick Demo Login"** for instant offline access.
- *Once logged in, you will be taken directly to your Dashboard.*

---

## 2. Dashboard & Baki / Jama Tracking
The Dashboard provides an instant snapshot of your retail business finances:

- **Filter Period**: Tap on any time filter chip at the top:
  - **Today**: See today's sales and collections.
  - **This Week**: Weekly summary.
  - **This Month**: Current calendar month.
  - **This Year**: Year-to-date business totals.
  - **Custom Range**: Pick any start and end date.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - 📋 **Total Bills**: Total count of invoices in the period.
  - 🟢 **Jama (Paid Amount)**: Total money received in cash/UPI.
  - 🔴 **Baki (Outstanding Due)**: Total pending balance to be collected.
- **Recent Invoices**: Shows the last 5 bills created. Tap on any invoice to view full details.

---

## 3. Managing Customers (Sale Directory)
- Tap the **Customers** tab at the bottom.
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find any customer by name or phone number.
- **Add Customer**: Tap the blue **(+) button** at the bottom right. Enter their Name, Phone number, and Address, then tap **Save**.
- **Customer Ledger**: Tap on any customer to see their total purchase amount, Jama, Baki, and full list of invoices.

---

## 4. Managing Buyers / Suppliers (Purchase Directory)
- Tap the **Buyers** tab at the bottom.
- Use this section to maintain supplier contacts and wholesale purchase records.
- Tap the purple **(+) button** to add a new supplier/buyer with Name, Phone, and Address.

---

## 5. Creating a Bill / Invoice
To create a new invoice:
1. Tap the **"New Bill / Invoice"** card on the Dashboard, or the **(+)** button inside a customer's ledger.
2. Select **Sale (Customer)** or **Purchase (Buyer)**.
3. Select the customer from the dropdown, or tap **"+ Add New"** to create a customer on the spot.
4. Set the **Invoice Date** and optional **Due Date**.
5. Add items:
   - Enter **Item Description** (e.g., *Rice 5kg*, *Oil 1L*).
   - Enter **Quantity** and **Price / Rate (₹)**.
   - The line total is automatically calculated.
   - Tap **"+ Add Item"** to add more products.
6. Enter a **Discount (₹)** if applicable.
7. Enter any optional **Notes / Terms**.
8. Tap **Save Invoice**.

---

## 6. Save & Sharing Options (WhatsApp & Print)
Immediately after saving an invoice, a popup window will appear with three instant options:

1. 📤 **Send via WhatsApp**: Generates a clean PDF bill and opens WhatsApp to send to the customer.
2. 🖨️ **Print Invoice**: Opens the printer dialog to wirelessly print via Bluetooth or AirPrint.
3. ⬅️ **Back to List**: Takes you back to your invoice list.

---

## 7. Recording Partial or Full Payments
When a customer makes a payment towards an outstanding bill:
1. Open the invoice from the Dashboard or Customer page.
2. Under the **Payment History** section, tap **"+ Record Payment"**.
3. Enter the **Payment Amount (₹)**, select the method (**Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer**), and tap **Save**.
4. The bill's Baki and status will update automatically.

- Tap the **Power / Logout Icon** in the top left of the Dashboard header.
